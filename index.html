Days, weeks, or months in between

Guidance from that one hour fades, situations come up that no one anticipated, and the next session starts from memory. MentalCompanion is built for all the time in between.

How it works

From one session to everything in between, and back again.

  1. Your therapist's guidance comes in

    From AI session notes, or notes your therapist already takes in the tools they use today.

  2. It becomes a plan you can follow

    Concrete daily steps and reminders. When something comes up that the plan didn't cover, the companion helps you through it, building on what your therapist set.

  3. Your therapist sees how it went

    What came up, what helped, and what happened next. The next session starts from what really happened, not from memory.
